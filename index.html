Our Story

Born from a love of slow, honest bread.

The Flour Garden began in a tiny corner shop with one wood-fired oven and a stubborn belief: that good bread takes time. A decade later, we still mix every dough by hand and let our wild yeast work overnight.

Our flour is stone-milled by a family farm two hours away, our butter is churned locally, and nothing leaves the kitchen we wouldn't serve at our own table.
